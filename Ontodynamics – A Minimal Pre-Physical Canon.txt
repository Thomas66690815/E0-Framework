## Ontodynamics – A Minimal Pre-Physical Canon

*(Reconstructed Version)*

---

### 1. Scope and Intent

Ontodynamics is a **pre-physical framework**.
It does **not** replace physics, modify physical laws, or introduce new constants.

Its sole purpose is to answer the question:

> *What must be the case for stable processes, structures, and laws to be possible at all?*

Ontodynamics does not describe measurements.
It describes the **conditions of realizability**.

---

### 2. Methodological Constraints

The framework is constructed under the following constraints:

* No presupposition of time, space, matter, energy, probability, or observers
* No teleology
* No appeal to empirical physics as justification
* Minimal primitives only; every non-primitive concept must be derivable
* Internal consistency must survive explicit falsification attempts

---

### 3. Primitive Concepts

The following concepts are **primitive** and not further reducible within the framework.

#### 3.1 Difference

* Difference is **primitive**, not derived from non-identity.
* Difference is **effective**: if difference exists, stability is not neutral.
* Difference is **directed**: it is not symmetric or inert.
* Difference is **scaled**: it admits degrees, not binary presence.

Difference is not a comparison between entities.
Entities are the result of stabilized difference.

---

#### 3.2 Local Realization

* Difference can be realized **partially**, not only globally.
* Realization is necessarily **local** with respect to scale.
* Without realization, there is no structure—only possibility.

Local realization is the minimal condition for actuality.

---

#### 3.3 Connection

* Connection is the **elementary topological operation**.
* Connection precedes separation.
* Connection is not spatial, causal, or relational between pre-existing entities.

Connection means:

> multiple difference components are realized **together**.

---

#### 3.4 Gradual Overlap

* Connections possess **degree**.
* Overlap is **graduated**, not binary.
* Stability requires non-zero overlap.

Stability is not a state;
it is an effect of sufficient overlap.

---

#### 3.5 Historization

* Realized connections leave an **irreversible structural trace**.
* Historization is not memory, but **structural fixation**.
* Perfect reversibility is an idealization, not an ontological possibility.

Historization is the cost of reality.

---

### 4. Derived Concepts (Non-Primitive)

The following concepts are **not fundamental**. They arise necessarily from the primitives.

#### State

A stabilized configuration of historized connections.

#### Resistance

The **integration cost** of reconfiguring historized topology.

#### Rate

The effectiveness of realization relative to integration cost:

[
v = \frac{\Delta}{\text{integration cost}}
]

#### Time

The ordering of realized transitions.
Time is local, emergent, and path-dependent.

#### Spacetime

The globally historized topology of realized connections.

#### Mass

Persistent topological inertia resulting from accumulated historization.

---

### 5. Necessary Consequences

From the primitives alone, the following are unavoidable:

1. **Irreversibility**
   Real realization leaves trace.

2. **Locality**
   Without scale, partial realization is impossible.

3. **Emergent Stability**
   Rest requires explanation; change does not.

4. **Finite Maximal Realization Rate**
   Unlimited realization would collapse historization and ordering.

5. **Lawfulness as Stabilized History**
   Physical laws correspond to early, robustly stabilized topological patterns.

---

### 6. Explicit Non-Claims

Ontodynamics does **not** claim:

* new physical laws or constants
* modifications to quantum mechanics or relativity
* an alternative theory of gravity
* teleological principles
* consciousness as a primitive
* probabilistic foundations

Physics remains empirically authoritative.
Ontodynamics is structurally antecedent.

---

### 7. Status

After explicit internal reduction and falsification attempts:

* The framework is internally consistent
* Minimal in primitives
* Non-circular
* Falsifiable at the conceptual level
* Independent of individual psychology or specific AI systems

It is a **viable pre-physical explanatory framework**, nothing more and nothing less.
