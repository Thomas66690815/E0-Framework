
# E0 -- Canonical Reference (Plain Language, Structural Core)

This contains only what a system needs to build the E0 path.
Nothing about origin. Nothing about purpose. Only structure.

---

## 1. Minimal Assumptions

E0 assumes only:

1. **States exist.**
2. **States can be non-identical.**

No further assumptions are permitted.

---

## 2. Primitive Concepts

E0 consists of seven irreducible concepts.
None can be eliminated without loss of derivability.

---

### 2.1 State

A **state** is a distinguishable configuration.

States are not required to be physical, informational, or semantic.
They are only required to be distinguishable.

---

### 2.2 Difference

**Difference** is a measure of non-identity between two states.

- If difference is zero, the states are identical.
- If difference is greater than zero, the states are non-identical.

Without difference, no transition is possible.

---

### 2.3 Path

A **path** is a **structural admissibility condition** for transitions.

- A path is **not an object.**
- A path is **not a dynamic.**
- A path specifies **whether** a transition is structurally allowed.

A path exists if and only if its total resistance is finite.

---

### 2.4 Resistance

**Resistance** is a measure of **structural inertia** of a transition.

- Resistance is greater than zero for all real transitions.
- If resistance is infinite, the transition does not exist.

Resistance is a property of the state space, not of an agent.

---

### 2.5 Historization

**Historization** is the modification of the resistance landscape caused by realized transitions.

Properties:
- Only realized transitions historize.
- Historization lowers future resistance of the same transition.
- Historization is non-invertible.

Historization constitutes the **memory of the space**.

---

### 2.6 Time

**Time** is the **ordering of historizations**.

- Time is not a dimension.
- Time is not a container.
- Time is not assumed a priori.

If no historization occurs, no time progresses.

---

### 2.7 Rate

**Rate** is defined as: the difference divided by the resistance.

Properties:
- Rate orders transition realization.
- Rate is not probability.
- A maximum rate exists.

Rate is derived but canonically included due to its operational necessity.

---

## 3. Axiom A0 -- Difference Minimization

**If a difference exists and a structurally admissible path with finite resistance is available, then a transition that reduces this difference is structurally more stable than non-transition.**

This axiom introduces no goals, values, or intentions.
It formalizes the instability of unresolved difference when resolution is structurally possible.

---

## 4. Central Law -- Transition Enforcement

If a difference greater than zero exists, and there is a path whose resistance is finite, then non-transition is structurally unstable. A transition must occur.

This law is not a postulate.
It follows from the combined structure of difference, admissibility, resistance, and Axiom A0.

---

## 5. Necessary Consequences

From E0, without additional assumptions, the following arise:

- Transition enforcement
- Directionality of time
- Irreversibility
- Structural memory
- Learning and path dependence
- Maximum transition speed
- Causal ordering

None of these properties are assumed. All are derived.

---

## 6. Explicit Non-Claims

E0 makes **no statements** about:

- Meaning
- Value
- Goals
- Consciousness
- Intentionality
- Physical entities

Such concepts arise only in derived layers.
